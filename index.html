    Welcome to ReelCheck. Point your camera at the TV, zoom until the title fills the box and tap Identify. Claude reads the screen and OMDb supplies IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic ratings.

    Both services need a key. They are stored only on this device.

    Get a free OMDb key — choose FREE, then click the activation link OMDb emails you. 1,000 lookups a day.

    Create an Anthropic key. ReelCheck uses Claude Haiku 5.5, the cheapest model; each identification costs a small fraction of a cent.

    When you tap Identify, the picture inside the box goes to Anthropic. Titles go to OMDb, and posters load from IMDb's image server (Amazon). Your keys and history stay on this phone.
